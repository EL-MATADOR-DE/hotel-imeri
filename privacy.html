Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Park Hotel Imeri. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask for personal information like your name or email unless you explicitly provide it to us (for example, through a contact form). However, to help us improve the website, we automatically collect certain usage data, which may include:

  • Your IP address (anonymized)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages you visit on Imeri and the time spent on them
  • Referring URLs

3. Third-Party Services (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to measure traffic and usage trends on Imeri. Google Analytics uses "cookies" (small text files placed on your device) to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior information in an anonymous form.

To learn more about how Google uses this data and how to opt-out, please visit the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to request access to, update, or delete the information we hold about you. If you wish to be informed about what personal data we hold or want it removed, please contact us.
